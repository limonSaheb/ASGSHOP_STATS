    Sep 29–30. Scheduled quarterly storage migration. Delivered bandwidth and request volume to students were unaffected, and cache hit rate returned to its normal range in the first days of October.

    07 - Key Insights

    A quarter that changed scale

    Eight takeaways from Q3 2026 - what the numbers say about where Apars Classroom stands, and where it is heading.

    Milestone

    609K monthly students - double the previous high

    Rolling 30-day active students peaked at 608,723 on Sep 9, against 299K at the end of Q2. The quarter closed at 532K, still 78% above where it began. This is a new baseline, not a spike.

    Signal

    Aug 16: the admission cycle landed

    Search clicks peaked on Aug 15, delivered bandwidth jumped 84% on Aug 16 and settled at nearly three times its July level, and the 30-day audience added 147K students in the following fortnight. The HSC 26 cohort moved into admission preparation on one weekend - and the edge absorbed it with no change in delivery reliability.

    Live

    311K live participants, 917 sessions

    Live classes went from a feature to a pillar. The Varsity 2026 batch alone drew 115K participants across 71 sessions - about 1,600 students per class - while Rhombus Parallel ran 149 sessions, the most of any programme.

    Engagement

    Three times the time on platform

    Average engagement per active student rose from 18m 56s to 57m 05s. Long-form live sessions and the exam system drove it: students are no longer browsing, they are sitting classes.

    Content

    Academic holds, Admission arrives

    The HSC Academic Program kept its lead at 13.9M views with a 19% bounce rate. The Admission Special Private Programme reached 9.1M views and 93K students within the quarter - the fastest-growing surface the platform has had.

    Acquisition

    Growth was earned, not bought

    Paid channels fell to 11.6% of arrivals (from 12.8%) while new students rose 84%. Direct and organic search carried 76% of first visits; 1.33M Google clicks came almost entirely from branded queries at a 39.7% click-through rate.

    Reach

    Dhaka 353K - and 64% from nine cities

    Dhaka grew 80% to 353K students, yet its share of the total barely moved. Chattogram (110K), Rajshahi (81K), Khulna (55K) and the rest of the top nine grew in step. The growth was national.

    Scale

    1.97 billion requests, 92.2% from cache

    The edge served 1.31 PB - 24% more than Q2 - for an audience 86% larger. Cache hit rate held at 92.2% across the quarter and reached 96% at the peak of August; origin response averaged 233 ms. September alone delivered 648 TB, a monthly record.
